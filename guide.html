Trust & Safety Guidelines

ReMarket connects peer buyers and sellers directly. Review these essential security rules to ensure safe and pleasant campus transactions.

🛡️

Golden Rule of Second-Hand Trading

Always inspect and verify the physical item in person before making any payment. Never transfer advance deposit tokens or shipping fees to strangers.

🛍️ Buyer Checklist & Safety

  • ✓
    Check Product Condition Thoroughly:

    Inspect for physical cracks, scratches, battery health, charging ports, and ensure accessories match the listing description.

  • ✓
    Verify Product Details & Authenticity:

    For phones and laptops, check original purchase bills, warranty cards, serial numbers, and sign out of Apple ID / Google accounts.

  • ✓
    Test Functionality on the Spot:

    Power on devices, test Wi-Fi, audio, and camera before concluding the handshake transaction.

📍 Safe Campus Meetup Points

  • ✓
    Meet in Public Daylight Spots:

    Choose well-lit, populated campus zones such as the Main Library foyer, College Canteen, Central Quadrangle, or Department reception.

  • ✓
    Avoid Isolated or Private Locations:

    Never agree to meet in unfamiliar off-campus alleys, secluded parking basements, or private rooms with strangers.

  • ✓
    Bring a Friend or Colleague:

    Whenever trading high-value electronics like MacBooks or smartphones, it is always recommended to have a peer accompany you.

🔒 Privacy & Credentials Protection

  • ✓
    Never Share Passwords or OTPs:

    ReMarket administrators will never request your passwords, banking PINs, email verification codes, or OTP numbers.

  • ✓
    Keep Financial Information Private:

    Do not disclose credit/debit card numbers, CVV codes, or net banking credentials under any circumstance.

  • ✓
    Wipe Personal Data Prior to Selling:

    Sellers must perform a factory reset on all electronic storage media, phones, and hard drives before handing them over.

⚠️ Fraud & Payment Alert

  • ✓
    Avoid Suspicious Payment Requests:

    Beware of counterfeit payment screenshots, fake courier tracking links, or requests to scan a QR code to "receive money" (QR codes are strictly for paying, never for receiving).

  • ✓
    Verify Instant Bank Credit:

    If paying via UPI or cash, confirm the balance credit notification in your actual banking application before handing over goods.

  • ✓
    Report Suspicious Listings Immediately:

    If an item seems too cheap to be genuine or a seller behaves suspiciously, inform the campus hackathon moderators immediately.

🔍 Quick Product Category Inspection Cheat-Sheet

💻 Laptops & Tech

Check battery cycle count, test every keyboard key, trackpad gestures, Wi-Fi speed, and hinge stiffness.

📱 Smartphones

Test touchscreen touch matrix, camera focus in low light, speakers, microphones, and SIM slot connectivity.

🚲 Bicycles

Inspect brake pads, gear shifter smoothness, tire tread depth, pedal bearings, and frame alignment.

📚 Textbooks

Verify edition year against your course syllabus, verify no missing pages, and check handwriting clarity.
